04 — Straight answers

The three things you always ask.

Will this wreck my natural nail?

No. We work in builder gel only — no drills, no e-file, no soak-off acetone bath unless you ask for it. Growth is removed with a hand file at the next appointment, so the plate underneath stays intact.

Do I pay a deposit to book?

Nothing online, nothing due now. You pay at the studio once the set is done — cash, bKash or card. Cancel free up to 12 hours before; after that we ask for ৳300 to hold the chair.

How long does a set actually last?

Three to four weeks for Standard and Statement, two to three for Petite. If anything lifts inside seven days, come back and we fix it free — that's the whole point of booking the same artist twice.
